## Design Context

### Users

Team Hub serves non-technical teammates, project leads, and the agents helping
them set up and understand a shared project home. Readers should be able to
recognize the hub's current state, see who owns an opt-in capability, and know
the next safe action without needing implementation knowledge.

### Brand Personality

Clear, grounded, and quietly confident. The voice is direct and human, with the
warmth of a well-kept field guide and the precision of an operations console.
It should make unfamiliar agent workflows feel understandable and accountable,
not magical or autonomous.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the existing warm editorial interface: tinted neutrals, restrained orange
and water-blue accents, bold Plus Jakarta Sans headings, and Azeret Mono for
small operational labels. Both light and dark themes are first-class. Prefer
plain-file diagrams, compact status rails, and visibly ordered sequences over
glossy product mockups, generic card grids, gradients, glass effects, or neon
"AI" styling.

### Design Principles

1. Show concrete states and handoffs instead of describing abstractions.
2. Make human ownership and opt-in controls visually unmistakable.
3. Use fictional, public-safe examples and label previews as previews.
4. Help a person and an assisting agent read the same next step differently but
   arrive at the same action.
5. Adapt the information hierarchy for phones; never hide a required setup
   decision just to make the layout fit.
